What we're building

A little less managing.
A lot more playing.

Different worlds need different setups. Quix aims to make it easier to keep them separate, discover what you love, and get back into the game.

▦

Everything in its place.

Create and manage separate Minecraft instances for vanilla, modded adventures and everything in between.

✦

Discover more.

Explore mods, modpacks, shaders and resource packs with planned Modrinth and CurseForge support.

↗

Keep control.

Manage updates and backups with clear choices instead of surprise changes to your setup.

⌘

Made to feel effortless.

Thoughtful details, a responsive interface and powerful tools without the unnecessary complexity.

∞

Built for what comes next.

Minecraft comes first, but the Quix identity leaves room to grow over time.
